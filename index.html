About

I am a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Saint Louis University. My current research interests are Larson's Problem, Change-Point Detection, and Existence of Meyer-type wavelets.

Research Interests

  • Applied Harmonic Analysis
  • Frame Theory
  • Change-Point Detection
